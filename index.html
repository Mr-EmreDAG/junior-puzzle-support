🧩 Junior Puzzle

Educational & Relaxing Jigsaw Puzzles for All Ages (4+ Family Friendly)

🛡️ 4+ Family Safe • COPPA & GDPR Privacy Compliant

1. Introduction & Privacy Commitment

At Dag Digital (Developer: Emre Dag), we take user and family privacy seriously. Junior Puzzle is a colorful, family-friendly jigsaw puzzle game designed to be safe and enjoyable for players of all ages (4+). We strictly adhere to international privacy standards including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Apple App Store & Google Play privacy guidelines.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Our game operates on-device and respects the privacy of all players:

  • No Account Creation: No registration, login, username, or email is ever required.
  • No Location Data: We never request or access GPS, coarse, or precise location data.
  • No Camera, Microphone, or Photo Library Access: The app does not access device photos, cameras, or microphones.
  • No Cross-App Tracking (ATT): We do not track users across third-party apps or websites and do not access advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID).
  • On-Device Progress: Sticker collection, settings, and puzzle completions are stored purely in local device storage.

3. Family-Safe Contextual Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free for everyone worldwide, the application integrates Google AdMob with strict family-safe and non-personalized configurations:

  • Configured with MaxAdContentRating = G (General Audience / All ages).
  • Configured with TagForChildDirectedTreatment = YES and TagForUnderAgeOfConsent = YES.
  • Zero Personalized Ads: All ad requests use nonPersonalizedAds = true. Advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID) are never used for behavioral profiling.
  • Clean Interface (No Banner Ads): Neither the main menu nor the gameplay screen contains banner ads, preventing accidental clicks during play. Full-screen ads are only shown after completing a puzzle when advancing to the next stage.

4. Parental Gate & External Links

All external links, settings, and privacy documentation within the app are protected behind a multi-language written arithmetic verification gate to prevent unintentional navigation outside the app.
